## Style Guide
- Format: "Fig. 1a" (abbreviated "Fig.", lowercase panel letters)
- Panel ranges: en-dash (Fig. 1a–d, not Fig. 1a-d)
- Multiple panels same figure: "Fig. 1a–d"
- Multiple figures: "Fig. 1e; Fig. 2c"
- Per Nature Neuroscience formatting guidelines
